Portfolios are collections of asset datasets that you can interact with. In the current release, you can upload CSV files of assets through the asset panel, visualize them on hazard or observation maps, and submit them for analysis. These portfolios are available for the duration of your browsing session.

In a future release, you will have the option to save your asset data persistently on the hub as a portfolio. Additionally, several sample portfolio pages will be provided. Tools to manage your asset portfolios will also be available in this section of the user interface.
